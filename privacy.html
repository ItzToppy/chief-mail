Chief mail privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

Chief mail has one user, the person who set it up. With their permission it reads their Gmail messages and Google Calendar events, read-only.

What happens to it

Code sorts most mail and events into a daily list on the owner's private dashboard. For mail from a short list of known senders, a masked excerpt goes to Anthropic's Claude API to be labelled. Links, codes, phone numbers, email addresses and long runs of digits are stripped out before it leaves.

Where it's kept

On the owner's own server. The sign-in tokens sit there in a folder only the reader can open.

Alerts to the owner's phone go through a push service and carry no names, amounts or subjects. Apart from those alerts and the excerpts above, it isn't shared with anyone. None of it is sold or used for advertising.

Removing access

Open myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Chief mail. Questions go to the contact address on Google's sign-in screen.
